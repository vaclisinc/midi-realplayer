midi-realplayer

A drop-in MIDI player for the web. One tag gives a MIDI file a DAW-style track view, a piano roll, a mute/solo mixer, and SoundFont sound. Audio recordings, such as the stems a transcription came from, play in step as extra tracks.

Many tracks

A five-track transcription

Tracks view by default; switch to Roll at the top right. Each track has mute, solo, volume and a SoundFont instrument menu. Space plays and pauses, clicking the canvas seeks, and Ctrl/⌘ + wheel zooms.

<midi-realplayer src="muscriptor.mid"></midi-realplayer>
MIDI + audio

Transcription next to the mix and its stems

Recordings sit above the MIDI tracks with their waveforms and share the MIDI's audio clock. Solo "Bass stem" and "Bass" to hear how well the bass was transcribed; mute every MIDI track to hear only the recordings.

Only one player on a page sounds at a time: starting one pauses the others. The SoundFont (GeneralUser GS) downloads on first play.

Example: a 20-second excerpt of "The Lost Mondays, Volume I" by The Georgia Sam Blues Band (Internet Archive, CC BY-NC-SA 2.5). Stems were separated with BS-RoFormer and each stem transcribed with MuScriptor; both are model output, not hand annotation.
