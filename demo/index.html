midi-realplayer

Designed for music researchers building demo pages and listening tests. Present single-track or multi-track MIDI, or play audio and MIDI together in sync. Let listeners explore individual parts and listen to MIDI alongside recordings, right in the browser.

One track

Press Play to hear the piano part. Switch to Roll to see the notes on a piano keyboard. The player adjusts its height to fit this single part.

<midi-realplayer src="piano.mid"></midi-realplayer>

Many tracks

Listen to all five parts together, or press S on a track to hear it on its own. M mutes a track, and the menu below its name changes the instrument sound. Switch between Tracks and Roll at the top right.

<midi-realplayer src="muscriptor.mid"></midi-realplayer>

MIDI + audio

Each instrument’s recording (stem) sits directly above its MIDI track. Press S on both “Bass stem” and “Bass” to compare them together, then mute either one to listen to the other. They stay synchronized as you play, pause or seek.

A few useful controls

Click anywhere in the notes or waveforms to jump to that point. Use + and − to zoom, and Fit to see the whole clip. The vertical slider changes row height; rows fit automatically until you adjust it yourself.

When the player has keyboard focus, Space plays or pauses. You can also zoom with Ctrl/⌘ + the mouse wheel. Export WAV saves the mix with your current mute, solo and volume settings.

Starting one player pauses the others. The first time you press Play, the player downloads its instrument sounds (GeneralUser GS); later players reuse them.

Example: a 20-second excerpt of "The Lost Mondays, Volume I" by The Georgia Sam Blues Band (Internet Archive, CC BY-NC-SA 2.5). The instrument recordings were separated with BS-RoFormer and converted to MIDI with MuScriptor using AMTFlow. These examples were generated by models and may contain errors.
